A MACHINE OUT OF FRAME

hors champ is an experimental sound interface that turns concepts, words and perceptual states into musical matter.

Neither a traditional DAW nor a simple generator, the app acts as a conceptual interpretation machine where every module becomes a gesture and every word becomes a texture.

Move between concept banks, shape intensities, switch cognitive modes and watch the prompt being built in real time through a DOS-style trace.

The app brings together AI sound generation, virtual synthesizers, drum machines, a looper, voice commands and MIDI controllers in a single performance environment. Designed for musicians and sonic explorers, it favours a fluid experience where the technology steps aside for creativity.

MODULES

Each module is one dimension of the conceptual interface, letting you sculpt sound through language and perceptual states.

01

Concepts

Words become matter.

Nine banks of cells (identity, silence, erasure…), three of them drawn from the manuscript Exister sans identité, plus a free bank for your own words.

02

Frame / Ambiance

Set the scene.

Overall musical context: minimal techno, dub techno, acid techno, krautrock, noise, neo-classical… or “No drum / ambient only”.

03

Sonic Texture

Sculpt the mood.

Emotional modulator: fragile, tension, isolation, confusion, calm, threat, melancholic, suspended…

04

Interpretation Modes

Shift the lens.

Eight lenses for reading a concept: literal, manifesto, bias, pressure, violence, safe space, dissociation, ritual calm.

05

RAW

Break the conventions.

Strips away stylistic colouring to reveal a drier, more abstract or unstable version of the preset.

06

Looper & Time

Compose in time.

Tempo, count-in, looper, layering of generative parts and recording your set.

07

Beat Lab

Build the rhythm.

16/32-step sequencer, drum kits, pads and breaks, and a chained effects rack ending in a tape machine.

08

Hydra Visuals

Turn sound into image.

Sound-reactive generative visuals that can be sent to a second screen during a performance.

09

Trace / Narration

Watch the machine think.

The prompt and sound decisions shown as a retro-computing log.

TRY IT

Hors Champ runs right in your browser. Nothing to install: open the app, plug in your headphones and play.

Open horschamp.ai.studio ↗ User guide (PDF, in French) ↗
01

Open the app

On a computer, in Chrome or Edge (MIDI and voice commands). Plug in your MIDI controller, then click “Detect inputs”. Every module starts switched off: turn on the ones you want with their ON button.

02

Your Gemini key

For AI sound generation, create a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey and paste it into the welcome window.

03

Or without AI

Choose “Continue without AI”: drum machine, synths, effects rack and visuals all work in local mode.

BETA VERSION — EXPERIMENTAL. Your key stays stored in your browser and is only used for your own requests; it is never kept on the server. AI usage counts against your own Gemini quota. Bugs may happen: tell me what works or breaks at info@kimmaurice.com.
